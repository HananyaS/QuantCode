## Rulings on the drafter's open decisions

**1. Replay universe = cache pools defined at the 09:30 open. Ruling: CHANGE (needs U1).**
The causal-screen-∩-cache rule is honoured as far as causality goes. Every C1 rule is known at 09:30, before the earliest fill (09:35). It is *not* yet a test of an implementable version of the user's design. That is because the 09:25 screen, as replayed, ranks its `top_n` and its cross-sectional percentiles (`screen_score`, PREREG:241) only among names that **will** pass a 09:30 open-gap rule. Live, a 09:25 screen ranks the whole filtered market. The two select different names (finding B1). The fix is to pre-register the gate now as an explicit formula G(D). The replayed system then is "09:25 pre-market scores, 09:30 gate G, `top_n` by score inside G". G3 implements that G, and only that G. Whether that variant is still "the user's design" is the user's call (U1).

**2. PRIOR_TRIALS = 350 as an upper bound. Ruling: CHANGE. Tabulate before the run, and only an increase is allowed.**
Nothing shows that 350 is an upper bound, and there is evidence that even 287 is incomplete (finding W7). Take N_prior = max(350, tally). Deflation grows only like √(2 ln N), so this is a warn, not a block. It still has to be a tally.

**3. Breakout-lab trials excluded. Ruling: ACCEPT, with a stated principle and a sensitivity.**
The family that counts is the searches whose winners could be chosen to answer *this* question on *this* data lineage. The breakout lab uses a different branch, a different data root (`data/research_breakout`) and a daily horizon, and R16's cell is never chosen in competition with a breakout spec. The principle must be written down, though, because the 287 already includes daily-strategy rounds that are no closer to R16 than the breakout lab is. Report DSR with the breakout ledger added as a sensitivity (E9).

**4. Holdout: CI only on net return; controls beaten by point estimate. Ruling: ACCEPT.**
This matches spec §4 ("CI that excludes zero, and it beats every control"). The dev gate already requires paired CIs against every control (PREREG:654-656). Requiring them again on about 250 days would be a pre-registered near-certain fail, and that would be dishonest in the other direction. Two edits are required: the holdout cost-refit year rule (E15) and a fuller contamination disclosure (W8 / E14).

**5. Within-spec choices. Ruling: ACCEPT all, with three edits.**
- GBDT + CNN: accepted. One level is chosen from each of the spec's pairs, with no data used. The torch determinism flags must be named (E18).
- Time stop = min(ts + h, 15:55) in every exit arm: accepted. It makes the exit factor a single-variable contrast (only the bracket changes), and it is what `risk.md:36` already does.
- ATR period 5: accepted, with the reason the document gives (equal entry sets across exit arms). That reason is incomplete, though. A zero ATR makes the bracket invalid, the entry is then skipped in the ATR arm only (`risk.md:50,140`), and the entry sets differ again. Floor the ATR at one tick (E10).
- `quote_size_frac` 0.5: accepted. It is stricter than the spec's "shares at the best quote". In replay it can bind only within 5 minutes of 09:34/09:35/15:55, and that has to be reported (it already is, at PREREG:509).
- $1-$10 and ADV ≥ $1M: accepted. They are justified by pool coverage, not by results. §4.5's description of what the universe actually contains is imprecise, though (W2 / E3).
- `top_n` 10: accepted, together with the binding-share disclosure (PREREG:259-263).

**6. Vectorised grid with an engine parity test. Ruling: ACCEPT, with a stricter parity specification.**
"Agrees" (PREREG:663) has no definition. 50 random days can miss every edge case. The arms the parity covers are not listed (W6 / E13).

**7. SPY presence in the daily cache. Ruling: CHANGE. Use the SPY file already on disk.**
SPY is excluded from `research_penny/daily` by how that cache was built (N2). `C:/Users/Shachar/Desktop/PythonProjects/QuantCode/data/cache/SPY.parquet` exists (verified by file listing; I did not read its contents). ## Findings, in checklist order

### 1. Adjustment basis

**[warn] W1. The pre-market basis is never checked, so a corporate action between the two fetches can make a fake `pm_gap` at the top of the screen.** *Suspected* (mechanism verified, frequency unknown).
`pm_gap = pm_close_last × f_{D-1} / close_unadj_{D-1} - 1` (PREREG:248) uses the daily cache's factor for D-1 on bars from the pre-market cache. The two caches were fetched at different times. If a split was applied between the fetches, the pre-market bars sit on a different basis from `f_{D-1}`, and `pm_gap` is off by the split ratio. Such a name ranks first. `feed.md:72-77` checks this for minute bars only, using D's row; the pre-market path has no check at all.
Evidence: PREREG:248; `research/intraday/docs/design/feed.md:72-77`; `research/intraday/docs/design/screener.md:84-89`.
Change: E5 (a basis check of the D-1 pre-market close against the D minute-cache open, known by 09:30, applied as a universe exclusion in every arm and counted).

**[note] N1. The share basis for participation is ambiguous.** §9.1 (PREREG:518-519) says "20-session median unadjusted share volume", and §4.4 converts each row with its own factor. For **share** counts, `risk.md:103-107` requires a single basis, `f_{D-1}`, so that a split inside the window does not mix bases. Dollar volume is fine either way. Change: E17.

### 2. Decision-time leakage

**[block] B1. The universe is gated on D's 09:30 opening print, but the gate is not pre-registered. It is deferred to G3, which comes after the result.** *Verified* (from the document text and the pool rules).
- The admissible minute universe is the C1 open-gap pools plus the R15 entry sessions (PREREG:138-141, 198-205). So the 09:25 screen's percentile ranks (PREREG:241) and its `top_n` cut are computed among names already conditioned on D's opening gap. That is information a 09:25 screen does not have. It is causal for the 09:35 trade, so this is not a return leak. But the replayed "screen" is not the screen the user specified, and a live system running the document's own §5.1 rule would select different names.
- §4.5 (PREREG:207-210) and §14.1 make a pass "conditional" on a gate that G3 will specify later. A gate chosen after H1 is read is a post-result design choice, and the replay result need not transfer to it.
- The pool strata are also narrower than §4.5 says. Round 4 was gap ≥ +3% **with ADV ≥ $5M**, and round 3 was gap ≥ +10% with ADV ≥ $100k (LOG.md:972). So gap-ups of +3% to +10% with ADV of $1M-$5M are absent, as is everything with |gap| < 3%.
Evidence: PREREG:138-141, 198-210, 727-730; `research/penny/LOG.md:970-983`; `research/penny/etl/candidates.py:8-12,65-71`.
Change: E1, E2, E3, E4 (write G(D) as an explicit formula now: admissible = G(D) ∩ cache ∩ C0/C1 provenance; percentiles and `top_n` are taken within G; G3 implements this G and may not redefine it). U1 also applies.

**[block] B2. `hyp_pool` is classed C0 ("the split flag is a pre-announced event"), but its split exclusion reads D's open and D's intraday high.** *Verified.*
`hypothesis_premarket.py:123` drops `any_split`. `build_universe.py:124-129` computes `any_split` with `flag_reverse_splits`, and `is_suspect_split` depends on `o2h`, D's open-to-high (`corporate_actions.py:98`). That bites for gap multiples ≥ 1.8 whose ratio error falls in [0.06, 0.12) (`RATIO_TOLERANCE = 0.12` against `RATIO_ONLY_TOLERANCE = 0.06`, `corporate_actions.py:49-60`). Among the largest overnight gaps, which are exactly the screen's top `pm_gap` names, the pool keeps the days that ran (o2h ≥ 35%) and drops the inert ones. That biases toward winners, and it lands on the screen arm. `screener.md:69-71` itself says these detectors read session data that is "unknowable at 09:25". The ADV band itself is C0. Separately, `forensic_universe` requires a finite D close (`build_universe.py:152-157`). That is a negligible same-day survivorship effect, but it should be recorded.
Evidence: PREREG:142; `research/penny/scripts/hypothesis_premarket.py:122-124`; `research/penny/scripts/build_universe.py:124-129,152-157`; `research/penny/etl/corporate_actions.py:49-60,94-105`.
Change: E6 (`hyp_pool` rows with `open_unadj / prev_close_unadj ≥ 1.8` are C2, and the rest are C0).

**[warn] W2. `hyp_pool` is described as part of the minute universe, but it is a pre-market pool.** *Verified in part.* `hypothesis_premarket.py` reads only the pre-market cache, and its docstring (lines 26-31) says 09:35 "would need minute bars for 420k symbol-days", so minute bars were not fetched for this pool. Arithmetic on the document's own counts agrees: 420,446 rows over 379 sessions is about 1,109 a day, more than the minute cache's maximum of 819 a day (PREREG:121). §4.5's "(from 2025-02) a broad ADV-band pool" (PREREG:200) therefore misdescribes the minute universe. Change: E3 (report the minute and pre-market attributions separately).

**[warn] W3. Admitting C1∧C2 symbol-days is sound only if the C1 pool was fetched completely, and that is not checked.** *Suspected.* The argument "the C1 pool would have put it in the cache anyway" (PREREG:164-167) is correct, and excluding C1∧C2 days would itself select on the outcome. But it holds only if every pool member was requested. Round 3 minute coverage was 82.7% and round 4's 92.2% (LOG.md:978). `fetch_intraday.py:130-138` writes no `requested.json` on a failed or empty session, and `cached_symbols` (lines 66-80) falls back to the symbols that were *returned*, so "requested" is unreliable on those days. The R5 and R15 builders are not in the repository (a repo-wide grep finds no producer of `candidates_r15` or `candidates_rev`). Their rules cannot be checked against code.
Evidence: PREREG:159-168; `research/penny/scripts/fetch_intraday.py:66-80,130-159`; `research/penny/LOG.md:978`.
Change: E7 (a reproducibility check for each pool, and a completeness check for each pool and year; a pool that fails cannot rescue a C2 or U symbol-day).

**[warn] W4. The in-fold entry threshold is the 0.90 quantile of in-sample training scores.** *Suspected effect.* GBDT scores on its own training windows are over-confident. A threshold set there is too high out of sample, so the model enters far less often than 10% of windows. That breaks the "matched turnover" premise of Main − C_b (PREREG:333, 463-466). Also, the threshold is set on the no-selection population and applied to screened names. Change: E11 (use the inner validation block, and report the realised eligible-window rate per arm).

**[note] N3. `c_label` and the cost refit use quotes from all dev years, including later test folds.** These are costs only, so no return leaks. It is a global constant in the training label. It is acceptable, and must be stated as such (E15).

### 3. Bar timestamp semantics

Checked and consistent: Window ts is the end of the newest bar; the fill is at the open of the bar with bar_start = T; a bracket triggers at a bar's ts and fills at the next open; the time stop fills at the open of the bar starting at T + h. All of this agrees with `execution.md:40-50` and `state.md:19-21`, and the label (PREREG:303-305) uses the same prices.

**[warn] W5. The label's missing-bar rule differs from the execution rule (a train/serve mismatch).** *Verified (text).* The label drops windows with no bar at exactly T + h (PREREG:309). The engine fills at the **next existing** bar (`execution.md:54-55`), and an entry expires after 2 minutes. Training therefore leaves out exactly the illiquid windows that the replay trades. Change: E12.

### 5. The account, not per-trade results
